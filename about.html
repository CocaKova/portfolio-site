About

I'm a builder. L2 IT support by day, local AI lab operator by night. I build and document infrastructure that puts privacy and sovereignty first — no cloud dependencies, no telemetry, full control.

My focus is on making private AI accessible and practical. I run a full DGX Spark lab (121.7 GB unified memory, ARM64) with Hermes Agent, Proxmox, Home Assistant, and various GPU-accelerated services. Everything runs locally.

  • ▸ Infrastructure tooling — dashboards, automation, home lab management (Proxmox, Home Assistant, Tesla)
  • ▸ Mobile development — native Android apps (Kotlin/Compose), primarily for Matrix/chat clients
  • ▸ Technical documentation — lab writeups, benchmarks, and case studies
